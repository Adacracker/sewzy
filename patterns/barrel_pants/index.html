Fabric and supplies

Medium-weight wovens with drape: linen, linen-cotton, washed twill, tencel, viscose crepe.

  • 1.5 in non-roll elastic, waist minus 2 in
  • Matching thread, needle 80/12
  • Safety pin or bodkin

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included; hem 1.25 in; paperbag casing included

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size layer (choose by HIP), tape the tiles, cut 2 FRONT and 2 BACK as mirrored pairs and 4 POCKET BAGS. Transfer the notches (pocket opening, crotch) and the waist, lengthen/shorten and hem lines. Cut your elastic: waist minus 2 in.
  2. Pockets — Pin one pocket bag to each front and back side seam between the notches (4–10.5 in below the waist line, clear of the casing), right sides together; stitch 1/4 in, press the bags away from the leg. (Skip this step for a pocketless pair.)
  3. Outseams — Front to back, right sides together, stitch the side seams 3/8 in from waist to hem — sew around the pocket bags in one go (pivot at the notches, stitch the bag edges together). Press seams toward the back, bags toward the front.
  4. Inseams — Fold each leg right sides together and stitch the inseam 3/8 in from hem to crotch point (the back inseam is a touch shorter — ease the front to it). Press open.
  5. Crotch seam — Turn one leg right side out and slip it inside the other (right sides together), matching inseams and notches. Stitch the whole crotch curve from the front waist edge to the back waist edge, 3/8 in; stitch a second time over the curve for strength, clip and press.
  6. Paperbag casing — Finish the top raw edge. Fold the top edge down 3.75 in to the inside along the waist line and press. Stitch a line 1.5 in from the fold (this is the ruffle), then a second line 1.75 in below it — the finished edge sits 1/2 in below this line — leaving a 2 in gap at the centre back for the casing. Thread the 1.5 in elastic through, overlap the ends 1 in, stitch, close the gap. Distribute the gathers.
  7. Hem — Press the hem under 3/8 in, then fold on the HEM FOLD line (1 1/4 in) and topstitch. Press the whole pair and let the barrel shape settle.
