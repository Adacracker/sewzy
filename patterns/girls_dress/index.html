Fabric and supplies

Light to medium wovens: quilting cotton, lawn, double gauze, linen, chambray, seersucker, needlecord. Not stretch.

  • 1.5 in bias binding (make your own or buy 1/2 in double-fold)
  • 1 button, 1/2 in + 4 in of 1/8 in elastic cord
  • Thread

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included; 1 in hem included.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size layer (choose by CHEST), tape the tiles, cut 1 FRONT and 1 BACK on the fold and 2 POCKETS if you like. Cut the bias binding 1.5 in wide to the length in the size key and 4 in of elastic cord for the loop. Transfer the notches, the hem line, the pocket box and the KEYHOLE mark.
  2. Keyhole — On the BACK, slash the fold line down from the neck the marked length. Face the slit: lay a 2 in wide bias strip right sides together over the slit, stitch a narrow V 1/8 in from the cut, clip to the point, turn the strip to the inside and topstitch. Fold the elastic cord into a loop and baste it to one side of the neck edge at the top of the slit.
  3. Shoulders — Front to back at the shoulders, right sides together, 3/8 in. Press open (or finish and press to the back).
  4. Bind the neck — Open the binding, pin it right sides together round the neck edge (starting and ending at the keyhole, catching the loop), stitch on the first fold 3/8 in, trim, fold the binding fully to the inside and topstitch. Sew the button opposite the loop.
  5. Pockets — Fold the pocket facing under on the FACING FOLD, press 3/8 in under on the other three sides, topstitch the facing. Pin each pocket in its box on the FRONT and edgestitch the sides and bottom, backstitching at the top corners.
  6. Side seams — Front to back, right sides together: stitch each side seam from the underarm notch to the hem 3/8 in. Press open.
  7. Bind the armholes — Join each armhole binding into a loop the length of the armhole, then attach exactly like the neck: right sides together on the first fold, trim, turn fully inside, topstitch.
  8. Hem — Press the hem under 3/8 in then on the HEM FOLD line (1 in total); topstitch. Press.
