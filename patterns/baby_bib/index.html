Fabric and supplies

Front: quilting cotton, double gauze or flannel. Back: terry towelling, flannel or minky for absorbency. Optional middle layer of flannel or PUL (waterproof).

  • 1 KAM plastic snap (size 20) per bib, or 1 in of 3/4 in hook-and-loop
  • Matching thread

Seam allowance: 1/4 in (6 mm) included.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size, cut 1 BIB front and 1 back on the fold (and 1 middle layer if using). Mark the SNAP dot and the turning gap.
  2. Sew — Front and back right sides together (middle layer on top), stitch all round 1/4 in, leaving the 2 in gap at the bottom. Clip the neck curve and notch the outer curves.
  3. Turn — Turn right side out through the gap, push out the tab, press. Fold the gap edges in and topstitch 1/8 in all round, closing the gap.
  4. Snap — Fit the snap at the marks: socket on the tab front, stud on the opposite shoulder back.
