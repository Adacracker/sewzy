Fabric and supplies

Light to medium wovens with drape: cotton lawn, linen, rayon challis, double gauze, chambray, seersucker. Avoid stiff or thick fabric — the gathers get bulky.

  • 1.5 in wide non-roll elastic, your waist minus 2 in
  • Matching thread
  • Safety pin or bodkin

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included; 2.75 in casing and 1.25 in hem included.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size, cut 2 SKIRT PANELS on the fold (one front, one back). Transfer the waist line, lengthen/shorten line and hem fold line. Cut the elastic: waist minus 2 in.
  2. Side seams — Panels right sides together, stitch both side seams 3/8 in from the waist edge to the hem. Finish the raw edges (zigzag or overlock) and press toward the back.
  3. Casing — Finish the top raw edge. Fold the top down 1/2 in, then 1.75 in along the WAIST LINE, press. Stitch close to the inner fold, leaving a 2 in gap at one side seam.
  4. Elastic — Thread the elastic through with a safety pin, keeping it flat. Overlap the ends 1 in, stitch a square, ease it back inside and close the gap. Distribute the gathers evenly.
  5. Hem — Press the hem under 3/8 in, then fold on the HEM FOLD line (1 1/4 in) and topstitch. Press the whole skirt.
  6. Optional pockets — Use the POCKET BAG from the Barrel Pants pattern in the side seams before step 2 — same method.
