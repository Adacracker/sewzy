Fabric and supplies

Outer: canvas, waxed cotton, cork, denim or quilting cotton with interfacing. Lining: laminated cotton, PUL or ripstop nylon so crumbs wipe out.

  • 1 snap or magnetic snap (5/8 in)
  • 1 in D-ring or swivel clip
  • Thread, 90/14 needle for canvas

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print the tiles and cut 2 POUCH outer, 2 POUCH lining (wipe- clean), 2 FLAP, the 3 x 6 in BELT LOOP strip and the 2 x 3 in D- RING TAB. Transfer the belt-loop box, the snap marks and the small corner squares (cut them out).
  2. Belt loop and tab — Fold the belt-loop strip in thirds lengthwise and topstitch; pin it to the right side of one POUCH outer inside the marked box, ends folded under, and stitch a rectangle at each end. Fold the tab through the D-ring and baste it to the side seam allowance of the same piece.
  3. Flap — FLAP pieces right sides together: stitch the curved edge 3/8 in, leave the straight edge open, clip, turn, topstitch. Fit the snap (or magnetic snap) at the mark on the underside. Baste the flap to the top edge of the BACK outer (the one with the belt loop), right sides together.
  4. Outer and lining — Outer pieces right sides together (flap sandwiched inside — it is narrower than the pouch so it clears the seams): stitch sides and bottom 3/8 in. Pinch each cut-out corner flat and stitch across for the 3/4 in boxed base. Repeat with the lining, leaving a 3 in gap in the bottom seam.
  5. Join — Slip the outer inside the lining, right sides together, match the side seams and stitch round the OPENING 3/8 in. Turn through the lining gap, close the gap, push the lining in, press and topstitch the opening 1/4 in. Fit the other half of the snap on the front.
  6. Use — Slide the belt loop over a belt or waistband, or clip the D-ring to a bag. Flip the flap open with one hand for treats; wipe the lining clean.
