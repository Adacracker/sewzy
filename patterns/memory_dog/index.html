Fabric and supplies

Shirts, dresses, sweatshirts, flannel, denim, fleece, baby clothes, blankets. Interface anything stretchy. Contrast foot pads and inner ears from a second garment.

  • Safety eyes (6 mm for the 6-10 in sizes, 9-12 mm above) or embroidered eyes
  • Black perle cotton no. 5 for the nose
  • Fibrefill; optional poly pellets for a floppy dog
  • Optional: 4 plastic doll joints or 4 shank buttons + strong thread
  • Doll needle, ladder-stitch thread

Seam allowance: 1/4 in (6 mm) included.

The steps

  1. Prepare the garment — Wash and press the shirt, dress or blanket. Cut it open along the seams into flat panels. Fuse lightweight woven interfacing to knits, jersey and thin cottons so they don't stretch. Decide where the pocket, buttons or a logo should land — the tummy (BODY FRONT) and the FOOT PADS are the favourites.
  2. Cut — Print with only your size layer on. Cut HEAD SIDE x2 (mirrored), HEAD GUSSET x1, BODY FRONT x2, BODY BACK x2, ARM x4 and LEG x4 (two mirrored pairs each), FOOT PAD x2, EAR x4 (2 outer + 2 inner), TAIL x2. Transfer every notch, the JOINT dots, the EYE marks and the GAP / OPENING lines.
  3. Head — HEAD SIDES right sides together: stitch the chin seam from the NOSE tip down the chin to the neck, then the short snout seam from the nose tip up to the BRIDGE notch. Pin the GUSSET between the two sides from the bridge notch over the crown to the nape, matching the side notches, and stitch both sides. Clip the curves and turn.
  4. Face and ears — Fit the safety eyes at the EYE marks (or embroider them later). Stuff the head firmly, nose first. Run a gathering thread round the neck edge, pull up and knot. EARS: sew one outer + one inner right sides together, turn flat, fold a small pleat at the base and ladder-stitch each ear between its notches so it hangs down beside the face. Embroider a black nose on the muzzle tip and a mouth.
  5. Body — BODY FRONTS right sides together: stitch the centre front. BODY BACKS: stitch the centre back, leaving the marked OPENING. Open out, place front on back right sides together and stitch all round from neck to neck. Turn through the opening.
  6. Arms and legs — Sew each pair right sides together leaving the GAP open. On the LEGS, sew the FOOT PAD into the bottom between the notches before turning. Turn, stuff and close the gaps with a ladder stitch — or leave the tops open if you use plastic doll joints.
  7. Join and finish — Stuff the body, close the opening. Ladder-stitch the gathered head to the neck opening. Attach the limbs at the JOINT dots: plastic doll joints, shank buttons with strong thread through the body (thread joints), or simply ladder-stitch them on. Tie a ribbon and, if you like, tuck a note into the pocket. TAIL: sew the two pieces right sides together leaving the base open, turn, stuff lightly and ladder-stitch it to the back seam just above the base.
