Fabric and supplies

Canvas, duck, home-dec cotton, corduroy, denim or a sturdy quilting cotton: KID 3/4 yd, STANDARD 1 yd, LARGE 1 1/4 yd (45 in). A contrast for the pocket if you like (1/4-1/3 yd).

  • Medium fusible interfacing for the handle (1/8 yd)
  • A pillow form in your size
  • Thread, clips

Seam allowance: 1/2 in (1.3 cm) included; hems fold 1/2 in then 1 1/4 in.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print with your size layer on and tape the tiles. FRONT, POCKET and BACK are half pieces — put the marked edge on the fold of your fabric. Cut 1 FRONT, 1 POCKET (outer or a contrast), 2 BACK, and the HANDLE strip by measurement (size key). Interface the handle strip. Transfer the pocket line and the two handle marks to the front.
  2. Hems — Pocket: fold the top edge 1/2 in to the wrong side, press, then fold again 1 1/4 in and press; topstitch close to the inner fold (a second row at 1/8 in from the edge looks sharp). Hem the top edge of both BACK pieces the same way — these two hemmed edges overlap to make the envelope opening.
  3. Pocket — Lay the pocket on the front, right sides up, hemmed edge on the POCKET line, sides and bottom flush. Baste 1/4 in from the edge down both sides and along the bottom. Optional: stitch a vertical line 2 1/2 in from one side to make a pen and phone slot; backstitch at the top.
  4. Handle — Fold the strip in half lengthwise right sides together, stitch the long edge 1/2 in, turn, press with the seam centred, topstitch both long edges. Pin the handle ends to the top edge of the front at the HANDLE marks, raw ends flush, the loop lying DOWN over the pillow; baste.
  5. Assemble — Front right side up. Lay one BACK right side down at the top (raw edge to the top edge), the other at the bottom — hemmed edges overlap in the middle. Pin all round and stitch 1/2 in around all four sides, backstitching over the handle ends. Clip the corners, zigzag or overlock the raw edges.
