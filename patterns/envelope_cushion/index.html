Fabric and supplies

Home-dec cotton, linen, canvas, velvet or an old curtain. Medium weight holds its shape best.

  • Matching thread
  • Optional: 2 buttons for the overlap

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included; 1.5 in hem on each BACK included.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size, cut 1 FRONT and 2 BACK. The BACK pieces are the same width as the front and a little over half its height.
  2. Hem the backs — On each BACK, fold one long edge 1/2 in then 1 in to the wrong side along the HEM FOLD line, press, topstitch.
  3. Layer — Lay the FRONT right side up. Place one BACK right side down on top, raw edges matching at the top, hem toward the middle. Place the second BACK right side down, raw edges matching at the bottom; the hems overlap by about 4 in.
  4. Sew — Pin and stitch all round 3/8 in. Stitch again over the overlap edges for strength. Clip the corners.
  5. Turn — Turn right side out through the envelope opening, poke out the corners, press, stuff the pad in.
