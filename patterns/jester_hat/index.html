Fabric and supplies

Wool or acrylic felt (no fraying), quilting cotton, velvet, satin, crushed velour. About 1/2 yd of each colour; 1/4 yd for the band and crown. Fusible interfacing on cotton points helps them hold shape.

  • 6 jingle bells (12–15 mm)
  • A handful of fibrefill
  • Optional 1/4 in elastic, head minus 2 in

Seam allowance: 3/8 in (1 cm) included on the points; band and circle measurements include it too. Finish the crown seam inside for cotton, velvet or satin.

The steps

  1. Cut — Print your size layer and cut 12 POINT pieces as 6 mirrored pairs (fabric folded right sides together): 3 pairs in each of 2 colours, or 2 in each of 3. Cut the HEAD BAND strip and the CROWN TOP circle by the cover measurements; interface the circle. Mark the circle edge and one long band edge into 6 equal parts.
  2. Sew the points — Place each pair right sides together and stitch both curved edges 3/8 in from the tip to the base, leaving the base open. Trim the tip, notch the outer curve, turn with a chopstick and press. Stuff the top 3 in lightly and hand-sew a bell to each tip.
  3. Head band — Join the short ends of the HEAD BAND right sides together (3/8 in), press open. Fold the loop in half lengthwise, wrong sides together, raw edges level, press. Optional snug fit: stitch a 1/2 in channel along the fold with a 1 in gap, thread 1/4 in elastic (head minus 2 in), join the ends, close the gap.
  4. Points onto the band — Pin the six points to the OUTSIDE of the band, tips hanging down, bases level with the band's raw top edge, one per marked sixth, colours alternating, bases meeting edge to edge. Baste 1/4 in — the stuffed tips stay clear of the seam.
  5. Crown top — Place the CROWN TOP circle right side down on the band, matching its marks to the points, and stitch round 3/8 in through circle, points and both band layers. Zigzag or overlock the allowance (skip for felt); press toward the crown.
  6. Finish — Flip the crown up and the band down: the band sits on the head, the crown closes the top and the six points flop outward with their bells. Topstitch the band 1/8 in below the seam if you like.
