Questions

Is Sewzy free?

Yes. Sewzy is free to download and includes 13 free sewing patterns, beginner lessons and 3 coach questions a day. Premium unlocks every pattern in every size, all courses and more coach help.

Do I need a printer?

A home printer works: print the PDF at 100% on US Letter or A4 and tape the pages together. Every pattern also has an A0 sheet you can print at a copy shop.

I've never sewn before. Is it for me?

Yes. Start with a first make like a scrunchie or drawstring bag, follow the picture steps, and use the lessons and coach whenever something is unclear.

What size porch goose do the outfits fit?

The goose outfits come in three sizes for 17, 23 and 27 inch lawn geese. Measure your goose's height, body and head, then pick the closest size.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Sewzy is available for iPhone and iPad.
